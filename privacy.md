    <h2>Information we collect</h2>
    <p>Micro Switch Guide does not provide user accounts, comment forms, contact forms, advertising, or email subscriptions. We do not intentionally collect personal information directly through this website.</p>

    <h2>Cookies and analytics</h2>
    <p>Micro Switch Guide uses Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager to understand how the site is used and to improve its guides. These services may process information such as pages viewed, interactions, referring pages, general device and browser details, approximate location, and online identifiers including cookie identifiers. We do not use this setup for advertising, user accounts, or selling personal information.</p>
    <p>Google processes analytics information under its own terms and privacy practices. You can learn more in <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google's Privacy Policy</a> and about <a href="https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11593727">Google Analytics privacy controls</a>. Browser settings and supported privacy tools can be used to limit or delete cookies.</p>

    <h2>Hosting and technical logs</h2>
    <p>The site is delivered through GitHub Pages and related internet infrastructure. Those service providers may process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time, to deliver the site, maintain security, and diagnose service problems. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.</p>

    <h2>External links</h2>
    <p>Guides may link to standards organizations, manufacturers, source material, or other third-party websites. Their privacy practices are not controlled by Micro Switch Guide. Review the destination site's notice before providing information there.</p>

    <h2>Changes to this notice</h2>

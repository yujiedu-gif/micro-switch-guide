## What the plasma cutting machine travel limit micro switch function describes

A micro switch is an electromechanical component that can change an electrical contact state in response to mechanical movement of its actuator. A miniature snap-action switch can serve this general position- or limit-detection purpose. In a plasma cutting machine, a switch used in a travel-limit arrangement would respond to mechanical movement associated with a boundary in the machine’s travel. The switch itself does not measure distance in the abstract: its operation follows physical interaction with its actuator.

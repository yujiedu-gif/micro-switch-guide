Calling a wire an “ignition wire” does not establish that it belongs to the high-current path, and calling a connection part of the starter system does not establish that it is a control connection. The circuit documentation must show what each part does.

This distinction is useful for interpreting a diagram, not for deciding where to attach a wire. No universal terminal layout follows from the general difference between control and power paths.

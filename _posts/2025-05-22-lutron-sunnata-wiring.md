## What to verify before relying on a wiring document

Use a documentation-first review. This is a way to identify whether information is applicable, not a wiring procedure or a declaration that an installation is safe.

- **Exact device identity:** Read the device’s own markings and compare them with the identity stated in its documentation. If the exact device cannot be identified, do not assume that a document for a similar-looking or similarly described item applies.
- **Document applicability:** Check whether the connection diagram and instructions explicitly cover that exact device and the equipment context under review. A generic diagram or an unverified search result is not enough to establish applicability.
- **Terminal markings and circuit function:** Confirm that the documentation identifies the relevant terminal markings and circuit function for the exact device. Do not derive either from terminal position, a sketch of another device, or a search term.
- **Circuit and load details:** Record the relevant circuit and load information for qualified review, then check it against the permissible load documented for the exact switch and equipment. Do not infer ratings or compatibility from a generic diagram.
- **Applicable requirements:** Check the equipment instructions and applicable local requirements. The requirements may depend on the work and jurisdiction; a checklist cannot resolve them or establish that a proposed connection is safe.

If any item is missing, ambiguous, or inconsistent, the documentation has not answered the question. Seek clarification through the applicable equipment documentation or qualified assistance rather than filling gaps with a generic diagram. Do not proceed on the basis of an assumed terminal arrangement, guessed rating, or unverified compatibility claim.

For electrical work in the United States workplace-rule context, the cited rule generally requires exposed live parts to be deenergized before work. It also requires a qualified person to verify the deenergized condition with test equipment, and limits work on energized parts to qualified persons. Verify all applicable requirements. These boundaries do not replace device-specific instructions or establish that any particular task or installation is safe.

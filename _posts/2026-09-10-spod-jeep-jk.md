## What to do when authoritative identification is unavailable

Treat the identifier as unresolved when authoritative documentation does not connect it to the exact equipment. Do not present a guess as a product identity, and do not describe an unverified item with specifications, wiring, ratings, or compatibility claims. A document establishing identity does not automatically establish electrical ratings or compatibility.

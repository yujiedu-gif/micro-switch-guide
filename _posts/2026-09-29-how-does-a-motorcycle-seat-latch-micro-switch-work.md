## How does a motorcycle seat latch micro switch work?

In a seat-latch design that uses a micro switch, the latch or a linked part moves the switch actuator. The actuator’s movement then changes the switch contacts. This is the basic mechanical-to-electrical sequence: latch movement, actuator movement, and a change in contact state.

The switch responds to the movement applied to its actuator, not to the abstract condition “seat latched” or “seat unlatched.” Whether a particular latch position moves the actuator, and how far or in which direction it moves, depends on the physical arrangement. The switch’s contact change therefore follows the actual mechanical interaction in that design. A latch position alone does not prove that the actuator moved or that the contacts changed state.

This description applies only where a micro switch is part of the latch design.

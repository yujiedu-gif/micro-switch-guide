## How should a reported endurance count be documented?

When recording or reviewing a life figure, preserve the count together with its source and stated scope. A practical checklist is:

- Is the figure identified as mechanical endurance, electrical endurance, or another kind of result?
- What electrical load is stated, if the result is electrical or the durability scope specifies one?
- What operating sequence and cycle count are given?
- What environmental or other test conditions are stated?
- Is the figure explicitly tied to MIL-PRF-8805K qualification and conformance tests, or does the source identify a different scope?
- Which relevant details are missing or unclear?

This checklist does not supply a rating or decide whether a switch is suitable. It helps prevent a test count from being separated from the conditions that make it interpretable. If a datasheet gives only a number, note that the available description does not establish the omitted test details. Avoid converting an operation count into years or a replacement interval without evidence supporting that conversion.

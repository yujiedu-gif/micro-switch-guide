## How is a starter solenoid switch different from a micro switch?

A **starter solenoid switch** is described by its association with a starter system. A **micro switch**, by contrast, denotes a miniature snap-action switch category. Under the terminology in MIL-PRF-8805K, a basic sensitive micro switch uses snap action, in which moving-contact speed is relatively independent of actuating-mechanism speed. This describes the switch’s operating principle; it does not identify a starter-system component.

The contrast is therefore about what each phrase tells you. The starter-related phrase gives a broad system context, while “micro switch” names a switch category with a snap-action operating principle. Neither phrase, on its own, proves how a particular circuit is arranged. In particular, the term “starter solenoid switch” does not establish that the component is a micro switch, and the term “micro switch” does not establish that a device is part of a starter system.

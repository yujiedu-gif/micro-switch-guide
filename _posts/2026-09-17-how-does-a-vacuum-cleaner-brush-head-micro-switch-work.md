## How does a vacuum cleaner brush head micro switch work?

In a brush-head design that uses a micro switch, the basic sequence is mechanical input, actuator movement, and contact-state change. A miniature snap-action switch can change an electrical contact state in response to mechanical actuator movement and is commonly used for position or limit detection. That general function explains the component’s role, not a particular brush head’s arrangement.

The brush head and the switch are separate parts of the system. The brush-head mechanism supplies movement; the switch’s actuator receives that movement.

## What a home-position signal does not prove

A reported home-position signal does not, by itself, prove that the slicer mechanism is physically at the intended home position. It indicates an electrical condition associated with the switch’s contact state. Whether that condition accurately corresponds to the intended position depends on the switch’s placement, mechanical actuation, wiring, and control logic.

Nor does the signal establish that the mechanism is aligned, has completed its travel, or is otherwise functioning correctly. The switch responds to the movement presented to its actuator. It does not independently confirm the condition of the full mechanism or surrounding equipment. A signal therefore cannot serve as a complete assessment of the slicer.

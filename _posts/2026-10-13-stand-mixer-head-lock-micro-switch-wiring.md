## What stand mixer head lock micro switch wiring describes

A micro switch is an electromechanical component. In a documented stand mixer design that uses one in a head-lock arrangement, the switch is associated with a mechanical input from the head or its linkage. The phrase “head lock” describes the application context; it does not turn the component into the mixer’s entire control system.

The relevant scope is the relationship among the head or linkage, the switch actuator, and the switch contacts. The rest of the appliance circuit is separate. A contact change may be part of a control path, but its effect depends on the particular circuit and design. That distinction matters when interpreting a wiring question. Identifying a switch or recognizing its location does not, by itself, reveal how the mixer is wired.

# Verifying a Keyboard’s Switch Type

The phrase “eagletec mechanical keyboard” is a search description, not enough information to identify the exact keyboard variant or the switches installed in it. To verify the switch type, first find the precise product identifier, then check documentation that explicitly applies to that identifier. If no exact evidence is available, leave the switch type unresolved rather than inferring it from the keyboard’s appearance, feel, or sound.

The same caution applies to construction, electrical ratings, hot-swap capability, replacement compatibility, and other specifications. Those details can vary between products that are described with similar wording. The steps below explain how to check what is supported without assuming a particular design or model.

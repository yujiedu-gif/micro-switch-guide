A **cylindrical grinder home position micro switch not working** symptom does not establish that the switch itself has failed. The grinder may not move the mechanism to the switch, the actuator may not be engaged, the switch contacts may not change state, or the resulting signal may be lost or interpreted differently elsewhere in the machine. Some grinder designs may use a micro switch for home-position detection; the supplied information does not establish that every grinder does.

A micro switch can change an electrical contact state in response to mechanical actuator movement and is commonly used for position or limit detection. That describes a component function, not the control logic of a particular grinder. Diagnose the mechanical input, the switch’s state, and the installed signal path as separate questions. Use the actual grinder and switch documentation to identify terminals and expected behavior. Do not infer those details from the symptom or from the switch’s physical appearance.

## Decide what the evidence supports before calling the micro switch faulty

State the result in terms of what was actually observed. If the mechanism did not reach or engage the actuator, the mechanical input path remains a question; the observation does not establish an internal contact failure. If the actuator was engaged but a documented, isolated check did not show the expected state change, that supports a switch-state concern under that test condition. It does not prove why the result occurred or establish the condition of the installed circuit.

If the switch state changed under the documented isolated check, the home-position symptom remains unexplained by that result alone. The wiring, terminal identification, control input, interpretation, or another machine fault may still need investigation. Use the grinder schematic and qualified service to determine what evidence is appropriate at those points. Do not infer a specific control response without the actual design information.

No single observation proves the entire grinder or switch assembly good or bad. Record the mechanism position, whether actuator engagement was observed, the test isolation and actuator state if a continuity check was permitted, and the behavior documented for the actual switch.

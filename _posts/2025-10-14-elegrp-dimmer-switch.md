## How is a dimmer function different from a microswitch?

At a general functional level, a dimmer is intended to adjust an electrical output associated with controlling a load. That description concerns the device’s broad function; it does not specify a particular circuit design, component arrangement, rating, or operating method. A microswitch, by contrast, is a switch mechanism. A miniature snap-action switch can change an electrical contact state in response to mechanical actuator movement and is commonly used for position or limit detection.

## Match the replacement micro switch to the application

Compare the original switch documentation with the proposed replacement before installation. The comparison should cover contact configuration, electrical ratings, actuator style, operating force, pretravel, overtravel, mounting dimensions, and terminal arrangement. The cited definitions distinguish several mechanical terms. Operating force is the force or torque applied to the actuating mechanism to operate the contacts. Release force is the force or torque to which force on the actuator must be reduced to permit the contacts to return to the unoperated position after operation. Pretravel is the distance or angle through which the actuator moves from free position to operating position. Overtravel is the distance or angle between the operating position and the extreme position to which the actuator may be moved. These definitions do not supply acceptable values for this machine; compare actual requirements against the applicable switch and machine specifications.

Electrical ratings are not universal switching capacities. Under MIL-PRF-8805K 6.1.1 and applicable specification sheets, rating values are requirements tied to stated load and test conditions.

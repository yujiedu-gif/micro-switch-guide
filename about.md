<section class="page-hero about-hero">

<section class="about-introduction">
  <div class="container about-intro-grid">
    <div class="about-lead">
      <p class="eyebrow">Why we publish</p>
      <h2>A small component can carry a large design responsibility.</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="about-prose">
      <p>Micro switches often sit at the point where mechanical movement becomes an electrical decision. A few millimetres of travel can affect safety, reliability, service life, and the behaviour of an entire product.</p>
      <p>Our purpose is to make that engineering knowledge easier to find and understand. We write for engineers, product designers, maintenance professionals, technical buyers, and anyone who needs to make a better-informed switch decision.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="about-scope">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-heading split-heading">
      <div>
        <p class="eyebrow">Editorial scope</p>
        <h2>From first principles to field problems.</h2>
      </div>
      <p>Each guide is intended to answer a practical question without requiring a product catalog to make sense of the explanation.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="principle-grid">
      <article class="principle-card">
        <span class="principle-number">01</span>
        <h3>Explain the fundamentals</h3>
        <p>Operating mechanisms, contact arrangements, force and travel terms, electrical ratings, sealing, and service life.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="principle-card">
        <span class="principle-number">02</span>
        <h3>Support better decisions</h3>
        <p>Selection criteria, application trade-offs, installation considerations, testing questions, and specification checks.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="principle-card">
        <span class="principle-number">03</span>
        <h3>Learn from real failure modes</h3>
        <p>Troubleshooting guidance that connects symptoms with mechanical, electrical, environmental, and integration causes.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="about-standards">
  <div class="container about-standards-grid">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">How we work</p>
      <h2>Practical, specific, and transparent.</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="standards-list">
      <div>
        <h3>Useful before promotional</h3>
        <p>Topics are chosen for the questions they help readers answer, not for the products they might promote.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <h3>Context before conclusions</h3>
        <p>Ratings and recommendations are explained with their operating conditions, limitations, and relevant trade-offs.</p>
      </div>
      <div>
        <h3>Sources that can be checked</h3>
        <p>Where appropriate, guides point readers to standards, technical documentation, and primary references for further verification.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="about-caution">
  <div class="container narrow-container">
    <p class="eyebrow">A note on engineering use</p>
    <h2>Good guidance still needs application-specific verification.</h2>
    <p>Micro Switch Guide is not a standards body or an independent testing laboratory. Before making a final design or safety decision, verify the applicable datasheet, standard, load conditions, environment, and regulatory requirements for your application.</p>
